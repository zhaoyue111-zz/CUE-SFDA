## Interpretation

- Engineering target mean Dice ≥0.38: E1@1000=False; H1-v1@1000=False.
- Q/K/V LoRA only; fixed `liver cancer` prompt; E1 and H1-v1 share frozen-source pseudo labels and 1000 updates. H1-v1 adds only the cross-case top-5 relation loss.
- GT is post-hoc only. No low branch, Stage 2, SAT, routing update, or dynamic QKV selection was used in these experiments.
- Evaluation uses the repository's 192³ sliding-window inference, 0.5 tile step and Gaussian blending.
- Per-case deltas are in `per_case_metrics_500_1000.csv`.
